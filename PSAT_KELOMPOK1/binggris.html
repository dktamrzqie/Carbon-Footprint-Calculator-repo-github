📖 User Instructions

Carbon Footprint Calculator Guide

Small Actions Can Make a Big diffeerence

  1. Open the Carbon Footprint Calculator Website
  2. Select a menu on website.
  3. Read the PAIBP learning materials available on the homepage.
  4. Scroll down to view and read the dialogue about encouraging the community to preserve nature.
  5. Select the "View Materials" menu or scroll down to explore other learning materials.
  6. Click "Environmental Figure biography" to read the biography of Jeremy Owen.
  7. Click "Carbon Footprint Worksheet and Validation" to view worksheet and calculation results.
  8. Click "History of Environmental Movements" to learn about the development of the industrial Revolution from 1.0 to 4.0
  9. Click "Graphs and Analysis Results" to view global temperature change graphs anf their analysis.
  10. Click "Technical terms Dictionary" to view technical vocalabunary in English along with brief narrative descriptions.
  11. Click "System Flowchart" to view the program Logic System Flowchart of the Carbon Footprint Calculator.
  12. Select the Carbon Calculator menu or scroll down to calculate the environmrntal impact of daily activities.
  13. Select the portfolio menu or scroll down to view evidence of collaboration among all subjects and project for developing a digital solution for environmenetal conservation.
  14. Select the Healthy Lifestyle and Ergonomic OHS (Occupational Health and Safety) Tips menu or scroll down to view healthy lifestyle recommendations.
  15. Click the link on the Healthy Lifestyle Tips page to watch videos about healthy living and the implementation of Occupational Health and Safety (OHS).
  16. Select the Logbook & Meeting Minutes menu or scroll down to view the role distribution logbook and group meeting minutes.
  17. Select the Wireframe menu or scroll down to view the wireframe design of the Carbon Footprint Calculator.
  18. Scroll down to view the footer containing an ethical message about environmental sustainability.

🎤 Presentation Script

Good morning everyone.

Today, i would like to explain how our Carbon Footprint Calculator works.

First, the user enters personal activity data, such as transportation usage, electricity consumption, and waste production

Next, the system processes the information using emission factors stored in the database

Then, the calculator estimates the amount of carbon emissions produced by each activity and calculates the total carbon footprint

After that, the result is displayed on the screen for the user to review

Finally, the application provides recommendations to help users reduce their carbon emissions and adopt more environmentally friendly habits.

This tool helps raise environmental awareness and encourages sustainable living.

Thank you for your attention
